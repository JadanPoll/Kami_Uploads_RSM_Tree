Questions, Tacits and Glossary are three entrances into the same knowledge. Open a question to read its answer; deeper investigations, technical maps and book passages remain accessible. A tacit challenge has an optional worked solution: try reasoning through the problem before opening it. A glossary term need not be linked to an existing question.

## Continuing the books

Follow [Operational2](https://github.com/JadanPoll/FKEN_REA_CaseStudies/blob/main/Operational2.md), [Operational2B](https://github.com/JadanPoll/FKEN_REA_CaseStudies/blob/main/Operational2B.md) and [Operation2Bminor](https://github.com/JadanPoll/FKEN_REA_CaseStudies/blob/main/Operation2Bminor.md).

Preserve natural question wording and stable IDs. Add answers that expose mechanisms and consequential relationships; avoid filling fields merely to satisfy a format. Applied challenges should compose knowledge for a real purpose, not enumerate topics. Keep source locators, evidence status and uncertainty accurate. The technical graph is a means of recovering and extending knowledge, not a prerequisite to reading it.
